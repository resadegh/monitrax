## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a **Restrained Editorial** aesthetic, tailored for a high-end Australian wealth operating system. It prioritises clarity, sophistication, and a sense of calm authority. Drawing inspiration from industry leaders like Mercury, Apple, Linear, and Stripe, the interface avoids decorative excess in favour of precision and intentionality.

The visual narrative is built on high-quality typography and a warm, organic foundation. It rejects the cold sterility of pure white and black, opting instead for a tactile, paper-like digital experience that feels both premium and approachable. This is a functional tool that presents complex financial data with the elegance of a high-end publication.

The product is the **operating system for Australian wealth** — it must feel premium, calm, trustworthy, and disciplined. Never childish. Never gamified. Never anxious. The user opens the app and feels in control, not behind.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a warm ivory base (`#FAFAF7`), providing a softer, more luxurious canvas than standard white. Primary text and dark structural elements use a deep navy (`#0B1220`) to maintain high contrast without the harshness of pure black.

**Emerald (`#16A34A`)** serves as the primary action color, used sparingly for high-impact CTAs, the primary chart accent, and positive trend indicators. Secondary information and metadata are rendered in **Slate-500 (`#64748B`)**. Never use bright neon green for the body — emerald is calibrated for restraint, not energy.

Red (`#DC2626`) is reserved for destructive actions and genuine critical states only — it must never be the default "down" indicator on a chart or delta chip. Negative deltas render in slate or amber depending on severity; saving red for "stop, this needs attention" preserves its emotional weight.

The **TRAIL Framework** provides a semantic spectrum for categorisation and status:
- **Sky** (`#0EA5E9`): Informational or auxiliary data, Track stage.
- **Amber** (`#F59E0B`): Cautionary states or pending actions, Reduce stage.
- **Indigo** (`#6366F1`): System-level insights or automated logic, Anchor stage.
- **Emerald** (`#16A34A`): Success, growth, primary activations, Invest stage.
- **Violet** (`#8B5CF6`): Premium features, specialised wealth tranches, Live stage.

## Typography

The system uses **Inter** exclusively, relying on precise weights and tight tracking for headlines to create an editorial feel. Headlines should never exceed a weight of 600 (Semibold), maintaining a sophisticated profile even at large scales. Numbers — especially currency values — should use the `data-xl` (40px / 600) or `data-lg` (28px / 600) tokens with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so columns of figures align cleanly.

Body text is optimised for long-form reading and data density with a generous 1.65 line-height. **Eyebrows** are a signature element: uppercase, 12px, weight 500, letter-spacing 0.18em — they provide clear section signposting without adding visual weight. Use eyebrows above section headlines, above metric labels, and above empty-state hero copy.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a **Fixed Grid** model for desktop to ensure content maintains its editorial composition. A 12-column system is used with 24px gutters and a 1280px max container width. Spacing follows a strict 4px / 8px incremental scale.

Generous whitespace is used between major sections (typically 48px on desktop) to emphasise the minimalist brand personality. On mobile, margins tighten to 20px, content reflows into a single-column stack, and headline sizes scale down to ensure legibility and prevent awkward line breaks.

The dashboard reads top-to-bottom as a single editorial column — TRAIL stage indicator → Money Story Hero → metrics row → diagnostic pairs → entity tabs. Tile groupings are organised in horizontal pairs (2-col grids) at desktop and stack vertically on mobile. Never crowd more than three tiles in a row.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is created through **Tonal Layers** and **Soft Warm Shadows**. Surfaces do not float in a void; they sit on the Ivory base.

Shadows are never neutral grey; they are tinted with the Deep Navy (`#0B1220`) at extremely low opacities (2–4%) to feel like natural ambient occlusion against the warm ivory base. For higher-level elements like modals or popovers, a secondary warm tint is added to the shadow to maintain the premium stationery feel.

The dashboard uses three elevation tiers:
- **Tier 0**: Background ivory (`#FAFAF7`). No shadow.
- **Tier 1**: Cards (`surface-container-lowest` = `#FFFFFF`). Soft 1px navy shadow at 4% opacity (`shadows.card`).
- **Tier 2**: Hover / focused card (`shadows.card-hover`). Subtle lift, 4px translation.
- **Tier 3**: Modals, popovers, command palette (`shadows.popover`). Pronounced lift, 32px ambient blur, navy at 8%.

## Shapes

The shape language is contemporary and refined. It uses a tiered corner radius system to create visual hierarchy:
- **Buttons and inputs**: 12px radius for a modern, accessible feel.
- **Cards and containers**: 16px radius to provide a soft framing for internal data.
- **Tiles inside cards** (chips, mini-summaries, segmented controls): 8px radius.
- **Pills / status chips**: full-pill (`9999px`).

All strokes — component borders, icons, chart axes, dividers — maintain a **1.5px** weight to ensure consistency across the UI. Never use 1px for borders on cards (too thin for the warm-ivory background; reads as a hairline glitch) nor 2px (too industrial).

## Charts & Data Visualisation

The chart language is intentionally **monochromatic with one accent**. Multi-colour pie charts and rainbow bar charts are banned — they make the UI look like a 2010-era admin template and overwhelm the user with chromatic noise.

Default treatments:
- **Line / area charts** (net worth trend, cashflow trend): emerald `#16A34A` line at 1.5px, area fill at 8% opacity, navy `#0B1220` axis labels at 11px slate-500. Reference dotted lines for projections at navy 30% opacity.
- **Bar charts** (entity comparison, monthly bars): emerald solid for positive bars, slate-400 for neutral. Negative bars use amber `#F59E0B`, not red.
- **Donut charts** (asset allocation): four-segment max. Use the TRAIL spectrum (Sky / Amber / Indigo / Emerald) for the four asset classes. Stroke width 30. Centre text shows the count, label below in slate-500 eyebrow.
- **Progress bars** (LVR, savings rate, emergency fund coverage): emerald fill against slate-100 track, 8px height, full-pill radius. Show the percentage label inline at the right edge.

Charts should always have a textual headline AND a numerical readout above them — the chart explains the trend, the readout proves the current state. Never show a chart without a number.

## Components

- **Buttons**: Primary buttons use the Emerald background with white text (12px radius, 14px font, weight 500). Secondary buttons use a 1.5px navy ghost border with navy text. Tertiary buttons are plain navy text with no border. Disabled state: 40% opacity, no hover. Loading state: spinner replaces label.
- **Cards**: 16px radius, white surface, 1.5px outline in `outline-variant` (`#E2E8F0`), `shadows.card` resting state, `shadows.card-hover` on hover. Internal padding: 24px desktop, 16px mobile. Card header uses an eyebrow above the headline-sm title.
- **Inputs**: 12px radius, 1.5px slate border, focus state uses 1.5px emerald border with no glow. Placeholder text uses Slate-500. Labels above (not floating) at label-md, capitalised.
- **Icons**: Use **Lucide** icons exclusively with a 1.5px stroke. Icons match the colour of the adjacent text. Size scale: 14px / 16px / 20px / 24px only. Never larger than 32px outside of empty-state hero glyphs.
- **Chips / pills**: Use the TRAIL framework colours at 10% opacity for backgrounds with 100% opacity text. Padding 4px / 8px, 11px text, weight 500. Examples: "Track" chip = Sky-10% bg + Sky text. "Active" chip = Emerald-10% bg + Emerald text.
- **Lists**: Use subtle 1px horizontal dividers in `outline-variant` (`#E2E8F0`, ~5% navy) to separate data rows without creating visual clutter. Row height minimum 56px to give content room to breathe.
- **Metric tile** (the building block of the dashboard): white card, 16px radius, padding 24px. Top: eyebrow label in slate-500. Middle: data-xl currency value in navy. Bottom: secondary line in slate-500 body-sm with an inline emerald (positive) or amber (negative) delta chip. Optional 60-day sparkline at the bottom right, emerald or amber, 1.5px stroke, no axes.
- **Paired metric tile** (Assets / Debts pattern from Copilot reference): a wider card containing two side-by-side metrics. Vertical 1px divider between them in `outline-variant`. Used on Accounts, My Wealth, My Safety Net hero rows. Each side has eyebrow + data-lg value + slate-500 helper line.
- **Time-period selector**: segmented control, full-pill background in `surface-container`, active segment is white with subtle shadow, inactive segments are slate-500 text. Always offers `1W · 1M · 3M · YTD · 1Y · ALL`.
- **Empty state**: ivory background, centred. Hero glyph at 64px in emerald or slate-300 (calm, never red). Eyebrow above. Headline-md primary line ("30 transactions to unlock intelligence", not "no data found"). Body-sm slate-500 secondary line. Single primary CTA button. **Always celebratory framing** — describe the next achievable action, never list what's missing.
- **TRAIL stage indicator** (Home page only): horizontal 5-step pip indicator. Each pip is a 24px circle: filled emerald for completed stages, outlined emerald 1.5px for current, slate-300 outlined for upcoming. Labels below in eyebrow style. Current-stage label is navy weight 600; others are slate-500 weight 400.
- **Money Story Hero**: full-width card spanning the content column. Three large lines stacked: "Earned · Kept · Free today". Each line: eyebrow label, then data-xl number, with inline keptMargin / freeDays delta in body-sm slate-500. No icons. No background colour — just white card with generous 32px padding. The TRAIL stage emphasis subtly highlights the line that matches the user's stage (T → Earned, R → Kept, A → Free today).
- **Sidebar (8-section TRAIL nav)**: Home · My Household · My Accounts · My Budget · My Safety Net · My Wealth · My Guide · Reports. Each row 44px tall, 16px icon + label-md text. Active row: emerald 8% background, navy bold text, 3px emerald accent bar on the left edge. Hover: surface-container-low background.
- **Bottom-of-sidebar hint cards** (2FA, "Start here"): small pill cards in surface-container with a subtle emerald or amber accent dot. Dismissible. Never modal. Self-hides once dismissed.

## Voice & Copy

Microcopy follows the TRAIL framework: warm, declarative, never clinical. "My Accounts" not "Portfolio". "Spending" not "Expenses". "My Safety Net" not "Emergency Reserves". "My Guide" not "Personal CFO". The Copilot reference does this well — "30 transactions to unlock intelligence" celebrates progress; never "Add at least 30 transactions to enable insights".

Numbers always carry their unit: `$1,247` not `1247`. Percentages are written tightly: `12.4%` not `12.4 %`. Dates use Australian short form: `27 May 2026` or `27 May` for recent items.

Never use emojis in production UI. Never use excitement punctuation ("!"). The product is a wealth operating system, not a notification app.
