# Inviting your first client

Adding a client to your firm in Monitrax is a four-step flow. You initiate, your client confirms, and the consent + scope plumbing happens automatically.

## Before you invite

A few things to have ready:

- **Your client's email address** — must be the one they use for Monitrax (or will use, if they don't have an account yet). They can't be invited to two emails simultaneously.
- **The data scopes you actually need** — request only what you need to do your job. Asking for FULL when you only need TAX looks aggressive and clients notice.
- **A short personal note** — clients are more likely to accept an invitation that doesn't read like a templated form. *"Hi Sarah, here's the Monitrax connection we discussed — Reza"* converts better than the default copy.

## The four-step flow

1. **Open Practice → Clients → Invite**
   Pre-populate the email field, choose the data scopes (or pick a profession-aware preset), add the personal note, send.
2. **Your client receives an email** with a one-click consent page. The consent page shows: who you are (your firm name + your name), what scopes you've requested, what each scope means in plain English, and how long the consent lasts.
3. **Your client reviews and approves** — they can adjust scopes downward (e.g. you asked for FULL, they grant TAX + DOCUMENTS only). They cannot adjust upward.
4. **The link goes live** — your client appears in your Practice dashboard. You see their book through the consent lens. They see a small chip on their own dashboard noting *"Reviewed by [Your Firm]"*.

## What your client sees about you

Your client always knows:

- **Your firm name and your name** are visible on their dashboard
- **Every time you view their data** is logged and accessible to them under Settings → Sharing
- **They can revoke access at any time** — it takes effect immediately, not at the next review
- **Conversations between you stay archived** for 7 years per AFSL requirements (this is disclosed at consent time)

Transparency is the whole foundation of the trust here. Don't try to obscure your activity — you can't, and clients can see anyway.

## Anti-poaching guardrail

Only an **Owner** of your organisation can invite professional seats (other advisers, brokers, accountants joining your firm). Admins can manage existing roster but cannot expand it. Every seat invitation is logged with the inviter's identity. This protects your client base from being exposed to a competitor adviser who shouldn't have visibility.

If you need to invite a new colleague to your seat roster and you're an Admin (not Owner), ask the Owner to do it.

## Plan limits

Each plan tier bundles a number of seats and clients:

| Tier | Seats included | Clients included | Add-on seat | Add-on client |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | 3 | 50 | $49/seat/mo | $2/client/mo |
| Practice | 10 | 250 | $39/seat/mo | $1.50/client/mo |
| Enterprise | 25 (configurable) | 1,000 (configurable) | Negotiated | Negotiated |

Inviting a client beyond your plan limit triggers an overflow charge — you'll see the impact in the invitation flow before you confirm.

## Common mistakes

- **Asking for FULL scope when you only need TAX.** Clients notice and lose trust.
- **Not adding a personal note.** Conversion drops by ~40% when the invite reads as templated.
- **Forgetting to tell the client to expect the email.** A 30-second heads-up call before sending lifts acceptance dramatically.
- **Inviting before the client has signed up to Monitrax.** Works, but the client has to do double registration. If you can, ask them to sign up first, then invite their existing account.

## What's next

Once your client accepts:

- Their data populates your Practice dashboard within minutes
- The alert stream starts surfacing their TRAIL stage / Health score / cashflow signals as they happen
- You can start a conversation thread (in-app or email-through-app) — both work, both archive together for compliance
- Your AI Guide gains access to their snapshot for general-information diagnosis (specific recommendations remain yours under your AFSL)
