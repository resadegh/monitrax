# Onboarding walkthrough

The setup wizard at `/onboarding` populates your account so the dashboard is useful from day one. This article walks the screens in order.

## How to start the wizard

- New users land here automatically after sign-up.
- To return: `/dashboard` → click **Resume setup** banner, or go directly to `/onboarding`.

## The screens

| # | Screen name | What it asks for | How to advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Welcome** | Your name, household type | Click **Continue** |
| 2 | **Household** | Adults, dependants, pets | Click **Next** (or **Skip**) |
| 3 | **Accounts** | Bank/saver/credit balances | **+ Add account** for each, then **Next** |
| 4 | **Properties** | Each property's address, value, loan, rental | **+ Add property**, then **Next** |
| 5 | **Loans** | Loans not already linked to a property | **+ Add loan**, then **Next** |
| 6 | **Investments** | Investment accounts + holdings | **+ Add account**, then **Next** |
| 7 | **Income & expenses** | Salary, recurring bills, typical spend | Fill the rows, then **Next** |
| 8 | **Final reveal** | Your dashboard preview | Click **Finish** to land on `/dashboard` |

## Common tasks

**To skip a screen:**
1. Click **Skip** at the bottom-left.
2. You can come back via Sidebar → **My Accounts** / **My Budget** / etc. and add the data later.

**To save and resume later:**
1. Close the tab — your draft auto-saves every few seconds.
2. Return via `/onboarding` or the **Resume setup** banner on `/dashboard`.

**To start over:**
1. Top-right of the wizard → **Start over**.
2. Confirm in the dialog.
3. You'll land on `/dashboard/setup` (the refinement engine, not the wizard).

## Common navigation questions

**Q: I closed the wizard. How do I get back to the same screen?**
`/onboarding` resumes at the last screen you saved. The wizard remembers your position.

**Q: I accidentally clicked "Skip" — can I go back?**
Yes — the **Back** button at the bottom-left of each screen returns to the previous one. Your data isn't lost.

**Q: Where are the data screens AFTER I finish the wizard?**
Same data, different surfaces:
- Accounts → Sidebar → **My Accounts** → **Balances**
- Properties → Sidebar → **My Wealth** → **Properties**
- Investments → Sidebar → **My Wealth** → **Investments**
- Income / Expenses → Sidebar → **My Budget** → **My Plan**
- Household → Sidebar → **My Household**

**Q: Will the wizard re-appear after I finish?**
No. Once you finish, `/dashboard` is your home. The wizard is a one-time flow.
