# The TRAIL framework

**TRAIL is the heart of Monitrax.** It's a five-stage journey that organises everything you see, do, and decide inside the app. Most financial apps treat every user the same. We don't — because *you're not the same person at every stage of your financial life*.

## The five stages

| Stage | Headline | What it means in your money |
|---|---|---|
| **T — Track** | Track your full picture | Every account, every loan, every dollar in and out — in one place. You can't fix what you can't see. |
| **R — Reduce** | Reduce the waste, fix the leaks | Positive cashflow held for 90+ days. Budget you actually stick to. Debt has a payoff date. |
| **A — Anchor** | Anchor your safety net | Emergency fund of 3+ months. Bills always on time. No surprise debt. |
| **I — Invest** | Invest in your future | Net worth growing quarter on quarter. Active investments. Property equity building. |
| **L — Live** | Live on your terms | Passive income covers more than half your expenses. Health score above 75. Real freedom. |

## Why it matters

The science is plain: **financial stress costs you about 13 IQ points** when you're inside it. Trying to learn investing while stressed about whether you can pay rent doesn't work. So Monitrax doesn't dump everything on you at once. The dashboard, the AI Guide, the recommendations — they all adapt to where you actually are right now.

If you're in **Track**, your dashboard celebrates connecting accounts and shows you the full picture for the first time.

If you're in **Reduce**, the same dashboard now spotlights cashflow, budget adherence, and the next dollar to save.

If you're in **Invest**, the same dashboard becomes about portfolio growth, equity, and tax efficiency.

Same data, different lens.

## How to know your stage

Open your **Home** dashboard. The TRAIL banner at the top shows you. The letter that's lit up is where you are. The letters before it are stages you've cleared. The letters after are what's coming.

You can tap any letter to read what that stage looks like. **You can't skip stages — but Monitrax never blocks you from looking ahead.** Adults decide.

## The science behind it

TRAIL is grounded in evidence:

- **Barefoot Investor (Scott Pape)** — 2 million Australian readers know this journey: Plant → Grow → Harvest. TRAIL is the digital embodiment.
- **Stages of Change (Prochaska & DiClemente, 1983)** — the same person behaves differently at different stages. A Track-stage user needs different help than an Invest-stage user.
- **Self-Efficacy (Bandura, 1977)** — small wins compound into confidence. Each TRAIL stage has *one* milestone, not a checklist of twenty.
- **Cognitive Tax (Mani et al., 2013, *Science*)** — financial stress is the enemy of clear thinking. The journey reduces overwhelm.
