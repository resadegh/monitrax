# Your Monitrax home

The dashboard is the landing screen at `/dashboard`. This article explains what's on the page and where each tile takes you.

## How to get here

- Click **Home** in the left sidebar (top-most item).
- Or click the Monitrax logo at the top of the sidebar.

## What's on the page

| Section | Where it is | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **TRAIL banner** | Top of page | Shows your current TRAIL stage (T / R / A / I / L). Click the banner to see what it means. |
| **Headline tiles** | Below banner — 4 tiles in a row | Net Worth, Monthly Cashflow, Emergency Fund coverage, Debt-Freedom date. Click any tile to drill into its source section. |
| **Today's three actions** | Centre of page | The AI Guide's top three suggestions. Click an action to expand it; click "Ask a follow-up" or "Ask a Professional" inside. |
| **Resume / Setup banner** | Top, only if onboarding incomplete | Pick up the wizard or start over. |
| **Help drawer** | Top-right `?` button | Opens this Help Center contextually for whichever page you're on. |
| **AI chat** | Bottom-right floating button | Opens the AI Guide chat panel. |

## Common tasks

**To drill into a tile:**
1. Click the tile (e.g. **Net Worth**).
2. You'll land on the section that owns that number (Net Worth → `/dashboard/balances`).

**To dismiss an action:**
1. Click the action card to expand.
2. Click **Dismiss** at the bottom-right of the card.
3. The Guide stops surfacing it.

**To see why your TRAIL stage changed:**
1. Click the TRAIL banner at the top of the page.
2. A drawer opens showing the rule that triggered the change + the next milestone.

## Common navigation questions

**Q: Where did the "My Accounts" tab go?**
Sidebar → **My Accounts** (3rd item from the top). Same place as before.

**Q: A tile shows "—" instead of a number — what now?**
That section needs data. Click the tile to drill in, or use the setup wizard at `/dashboard/setup`.

**Q: How do I get back to the dashboard from any page?**
**Home** in the sidebar, or the Monitrax logo at the top.
