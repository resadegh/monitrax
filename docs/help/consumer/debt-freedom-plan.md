# Debt Freedom tab

The Debt Freedom tab at `/dashboard/debt-planner` shows your debt-payoff projection. This article walks the page.

## How to get here

Sidebar → **My Budget** → **Debt Freedom** tab. (Third tab on the My Budget page.)

## What's on the page

| Section | Where it is | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategy toggle** | Top | Switch between **Avalanche** and **Snowball** |
| **Headline summary** | Top right | Debt-freedom date · Total interest · Months until debt-free |
| **Projection chart** | Centre | Both strategies plotted side-by-side; toggle to view single strategy |
| **Loans list** | Below chart | Every loan with balance, rate, repayment, and per-loan strategy actions |
| **Extra repayment input** | Top of loans list | Set a custom monthly extra payment to test scenarios |
| **Per-loan strategy** | Click a loan row | Open the loan's Strategy tab to model lump sums or rate changes |

## Common tasks

**To switch payoff strategy:**
1. Click **Avalanche** or **Snowball** at the top.
2. The chart + headline numbers update.

**To model an extra monthly payment:**
1. Find **Extra monthly repayment** input above the loans list.
2. Enter the amount → click **Apply**.
3. Chart + headline date update immediately.

**To model a one-off lump sum:**
1. Click the loan row → opens the loan detail dialog.
2. Open the **Strategy** tab.
3. **Lump sum** field → enter amount + date → **Calculate**.
4. The page shows months-saved + interest-saved.

**To change a loan's interest rate:**
1. Click the loan row → detail dialog.
2. Click **Edit** in the dialog footer.
3. Change **Interest rate** → Save.
4. Plan recomputes immediately.

**To exclude a loan from the plan:**
1. Click the loan row.
2. **Settings** tab → toggle **Include in debt-freedom plan**.
3. Save.

## Common navigation questions

**Q: Where do I add a new loan?**
Sidebar → **My Accounts** → **Balances** → **+ Add** → **Loan**. New loan appears in the Debt Freedom plan automatically.

**Q: What's the difference between Avalanche and Snowball?**
Avalanche prioritises highest-rate loans first. Snowball prioritises smallest-balance loans first. The chart shows both side-by-side so you can compare. (Tooltip available next to each strategy name.)

**Q: My PPR mortgage dominates the chart.**
Long-horizon home loans usually do. Toggle the loans list filter to show only non-mortgage debt for a clearer view of your shorter-term plan.

**Q: Where does my surplus from cashflow appear?**
The strategy uses your `/cashflow` surplus as the available extra repayment headroom. To override that assumption, use the **Extra monthly repayment** input.

**Q: Can I see what a refinance would do?**
Edit the loan → change the rate to your hypothetical new rate → save. Chart updates. (For a real refinance decision, use **Ask a Professional**.)
