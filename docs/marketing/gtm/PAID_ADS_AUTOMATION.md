## 1. Compliance gates (read first — these are hard blockers)

| Gate | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Google AU financial-services verification** | Since 30 Aug 2022, advertisers showing financial-services ads in Australia must be verified; unlicensed advertisers must obtain third-party verification of their **exemption** via Google's vendor G2. Monitrax holds no AFSL by design → exemption path. | 🔴 Not started — Step 6A.1 |
| **Meta financial-services verification** | Beneficiary/payer verification with AFSL information **or self-declared exemption**; eligible ads carry a clickable "Paid for By" disclaimer. | 🔴 Not started — Step 6A.1 |
| **Exemption basis memo** | The legal basis for "financial information service, no AFSL required" exemption claims on both platforms → bundle into the Q-GTM-5 fintech-lawyer brief (one engagement, two questions). | 🔴 Pending Q-GTM-5 engagement |
| **AFSL boundary in creative** | Every ad and landing claim respects `monitrax.com.au/legal/afsl-credit-tax-boundary-disclosure`: maths and mechanisms, never personal advice, never product recommendations, never implied licensing. Baked into the copy-generation system prompt (§3) AND human-checked at weekly approval. | Standing rule |
| **Data claims** | Any privacy/security claim in ads must match the privacy policy verbatim in substance (no data sales, Sydney-hosted, encrypted at rest). | Standing rule |
| **Q-DEC ordering** | Float→Decimal migration ships **before** any paid traffic reaches `/wealth-check` (wrong-cents on a result page paid traffic landed on = credibility kill). | 🔴 Pending (IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN Up Next) |

## 3. Step 6A.2 — Creative engine (automated)

Weekly n8n workflow (`ads-creative-batch`):
1. Claude (Anthropic API, prompts from Airtable `Content` library) drafts 6–10 ad variants per channel from the **website-copy SSOT** (`docs/marketing/TRAIL_WEBSITE_COPY.md`) — never freestyle claims. System prompt guardrails: no advice language, no outcome promises ("grow your wealth by X"), no urgency/scarcity, no shaming, warm-words rule (CLAUDE.md), AFSL boundary phrasing list.
2. Canva API renders static tiles from locked brand templates (Deep Cosmos system — no new tokens/colours ever generated by automation).
3. Batch lands in Reza's inbox: one email, approve/reject per variant by reply. **No creative ships unapproved.**
4. Approved variants pushed via Google Ads API / Meta Marketing API into paused ad sets (paused until 6A.5 ignition; post-ignition, into live rotation).

## 4. Step 6A.3 — Guardrail ops (automated)

- Platform-side **hard daily caps** (defence layer 1 — survives n8n outage).
- n8n rules (defence layer 2, nightly):
  - CPA > threshold for 3 consecutive days → pause ad set, flag in digest.
  - CTR bottom-quartile creative after ≥1k impressions → pause, promote best performer.
  - Spend anomaly (>120% of cap detected) → **kill switch**: pause all campaigns both platforms, urgent digest flag.
- Asymmetry rule: automation only ever pauses/reduces. Budget increases, new campaigns, new geos, new audiences → explicit Reza approval, every time.

## 5. Step 6A.4 — Reporting (automated)

- Nightly n8n pull: Google Ads + Meta spend/impressions/CTR/CPC + PostHog conversions → Airtable `AdPerformance`.
- New **ADS** section in the existing Founder Daily Digest: yesterday's spend, CPL, top/bottom creative, any guardrail events. No new dashboard, no new inbox.
- Weekly Claude-written summary with one recommended action (e.g. "kill variant C, shift $X to search campaign 2 — approve?").

## 6. Targeting (per Q-ICP-1 — wealth-builders, not broad PFM)

- **Google Search (primary):** high-intent AU terms — net worth tracker australia, investment property portfolio tracker, SMSF tracking app, property equity tracker, loan offset calculator app. Exact/phrase, AU geo, negative-keyword list maintained by n8n from search-terms report.
- **Meta/Instagram (secondary):** property-investor, SMSF, personal-finance-AU interest stacks; lookalikes seeded from broker-referred users (Phase 4 output) once ≥100 seeds exist.
- **Landing:** `/wealth-check` (post-Q-DEC) or waitlist page; PostHog conversion events as the single conversion truth.
