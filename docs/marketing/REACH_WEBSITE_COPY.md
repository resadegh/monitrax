## The Framework Section

### Section Headline
**Five steps. One journey. Your financial freedom.**

### Intro
REACH is a proven financial journey framework built into every part of Monitrax. It's based on the same principles used by Australia's Barefoot Investor, financial therapists, and the world's most successful financial planning methodologies — simplified into five steps anyone can follow.

### The Five Stages

---

#### R — Reveal
**See your full picture**

Connect your bank accounts and see everything in one place — your accounts, loans, income, spending, and recurring charges. No spreadsheets. No guessing. Just clarity.

Most people avoid looking at their finances because they're afraid of what they'll find. But here's the truth: the unknown is always scarier than the known. Once you can see it, you can change it.

> "I didn't realise I was spending $340 a month on subscriptions I'd forgotten about."

**What Monitrax does:** Connects to your bank via secure Open Banking (CDR), automatically imports your accounts, loans, and transactions, and shows you your complete financial picture in under 60 seconds.

---

#### E — Establish
**Build your foundation**

Before you can build wealth, you need a safety net. Establish means building an emergency fund that covers 3 months of expenses — so one unexpected car repair or medical bill doesn't send you into debt.

29% of Australian households can't raise $2,000 in a week for an emergency. Establish changes that. It's the foundation everything else is built on.

> "For the first time in my life, I have a buffer. I can actually sleep at night."

**What Monitrax does:** Tracks your emergency fund progress, calculates how many months of expenses you've covered, and gives you personalised recommendations to build your safety net faster.

---

#### A — Act
**Take control of your money**

This is where the magic happens. Set a budget. See where your money actually goes. Create a debt paydown plan that works — not the one that looks best on paper, but the one you'll actually stick to.

Research shows that paying off your smallest debt first (even if it's not the highest interest rate) is more effective because the quick win builds momentum. Monitrax uses this science to help you crush your debt.

> "I paid off my Afterpay in 2 months, then rolled that payment into my car loan. I'm saving $3,400 in interest."

**What Monitrax does:** Budget tracking, cashflow forecasting, AI-powered debt freedom planning (snowball vs avalanche — we recommend what works for YOU), and Australian tax optimisation.

---

#### C — Compound
**Grow and compound your wealth**

Einstein reportedly called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world." Now it's working for you. Track your property portfolio, investments, super, and assets — all growing together.

This is where your success equation comes alive: wealth going up, debt going down. Every property you buy, every investment you make, every dollar of equity you build — Monitrax tracks it all.

> "Watching my net worth grow from $180K to $420K over 18 months — with real numbers, not guesses — changed how I think about money."

**What Monitrax does:** Property tracking (equity, LVR, rental yield, depreciation), investment portfolio management, asset tracking, and net worth monitoring — all updated in real time from your connected accounts.

---

#### H — Harvest
**Reap your financial freedom**

This is the destination. Your passive income approaches your expenses. Your Financial Health score is high. Your debts are minimal or gone. You make money decisions from abundance, not stress.

Your AI-powered personal CFO shifts from "here's what to fix" to "here's how to optimise." You're not surviving anymore — you're thriving.

> "My CFO told me I'm 7 years from financial independence. I've never had a number before. Now I have a target."

**What Monitrax does:** Your personal AI CFO tracks your Financial Health score, surfaces your top 3 actions each week, forecasts your path to financial independence, and celebrates your progress along the way.
