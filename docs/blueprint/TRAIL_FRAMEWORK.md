## 1. Framework Overview

### What is TRAIL?

TRAIL is a 5-stage financial journey framework that guides users from financial confusion to financial freedom. Every feature, page, recommendation, and interaction in Monitrax maps to one of the five TRAIL stages.

```
T R A I L  to Financial Freedom

T — Track        "Track your full picture"
R — Reduce       "Reduce the waste, fix the leaks"
A — Anchor       "Anchor your safety net"
I — Invest       "Invest in your future"
L — Live         "Live on your terms"
```

### Why TRAIL Exists

Most people are financially stressed. Research shows:

- 72% of people feel stressed about money (APA, 2022)
- 33% of Australians report high financial stress (NAB, 2023)
- 29% of Australian households cannot raise $2,000 for an emergency (ABS, 2020)
- Financial stress reduces cognitive function by 13 IQ points (Mani et al., 2013, Science)
- Financial literacy alone explains only 0.1% of variance in financial behaviour (Fernandes et al., 2014)

People don't need another spreadsheet. They need a guide. TRAIL is that guide.

### The Core Belief

> **Success = Accumulating wealth + Reducing debt.**
>
> Every feature in Monitrax serves one of these two goals, organised into a journey that transforms an overwhelmed, avoidant user into a confident, thriving wealth builder.

### Why "TRAIL"?

A trail is a path through the wilderness. When you're lost in your finances, you need a trail to follow — not a map with 50 options, but a clear path forward.

Every trail has a beginning (where you are now), a series of markers (stages you pass through), and a destination (where you want to be). The TRAIL framework is Monitrax's path from financial stress to financial freedom.

### The Emotional Arc

```
😰 "I'm lost"  →  😤 "I'm fixing it"  →  😌 "I'm safe"  →  😊 "I'm growing"  →  😎 "I'm free"
     TRACK            REDUCE               ANCHOR             INVEST              LIVE
```

## 2. The Five Stages

### Stage 1: TRACK

**"Track your full picture"**

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it means** | Face your full financial reality — no judgment, just clarity. See every account, every debt, every dollar in and out. End the avoidance. |
| **Key question** | "What do I actually have, owe, earn, and spend?" |
| **Emotional shift** | From **avoidance** to **awareness** |
| **Milestone** | "I can see my complete financial picture in one place." |
| **Measurable** | All accounts connected via Basiq. Net worth calculated. Income and spending visible. |
| **Monitrax section** | My Accounts (Balances, Activity) — see Phase 36 simplification |

**Why it comes first:** Every proven financial framework starts with awareness. You cannot change what you cannot see. Financial avoidance — not opening bills, not checking balances, not facing debts — is the #1 predictor of low net worth (Klontz, 2011). Simply SEEING your finances reduces anxiety because avoidance amplifies fear of the unknown.

---

### Stage 2: REDUCE

**"Reduce the waste, fix the leaks"**

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it means** | Fix the immediate problems. Cut wasteful spending. Stop the leaks. Get your cashflow positive. Set a budget and start paying down debt. |
| **Key question** | "Am I spending less than I earn? Where can I cut waste?" |
| **Emotional shift** | From **overwhelm** to **empowerment** |
| **Milestone** | "I spend less than I earn. I know where every dollar goes. I've started my debt paydown plan." |
| **Measurable** | Positive monthly cashflow. Budget set and adherence above 70%. Debt paydown plan active. |
| **Monitrax section** | My Budget (Budget, Cashflow, Income, Spending, Debt Freedom, Tax) — Income and Spending are budget intentions, so they live in REDUCE, not TRACK (Phase 36) |

**Why REDUCE comes before ANCHOR:** You can't fill a bucket with a hole in it. If someone is spending more than they earn, telling them to save for an emergency fund is putting the cart before the horse. Fix the leak first, THEN fill the bucket. This aligns with the Barefoot Investor (Domino debts in Step 3, BEFORE building Mojo in Step 6) and Ramsey (Debt Snowball in Step 2, BEFORE full emergency fund in Step 3).

---

### Stage 3: ANCHOR

**"Anchor your safety net"**

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it means** | Now that your cashflow is positive, build a safety net. Create an emergency fund that covers 3 months of expenses. Anchor yourself so one unexpected bill doesn't send you backward. |
| **Key question** | "Can I handle a financial shock without going into debt?" |
| **Emotional shift** | From **fragile** to **stable** |
| **Milestone** | "I have 3 months of expenses saved. My bills are covered. I can sleep at night." |
| **Measurable** | Emergency fund reaches 3 months of expenses. Bills paid on time. No new consumer debt. |
| **Monitrax section** | Tracked through Financial Health score + Guide recommendations |

**Why ANCHOR matters:** Maslow's hierarchy applied to finance — you cannot focus on wealth building when survival is threatened. 29% of Australian households can't raise $2,000 for an emergency. An anchor keeps you stable when storms hit. This maps directly to the Barefoot Investor's "Mojo" bucket.

---

### Stage 4: INVEST

**"Invest in your future"**

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it means** | Now that you're stable and in control, start accumulating. Buy property. Invest in shares and super. Build assets. Grow your net worth deliberately. |
| **Key question** | "Is my net worth growing? Am I building something that lasts?" |
| **Emotional shift** | From **surviving** to **building** |
| **Milestone** | "My net worth is growing. I own property. I'm investing regularly." |
| **Measurable** | Net worth increasing quarter-on-quarter. Investment portfolio growing. Property equity building. |
| **Monitrax section** | My Wealth (Properties, Investments, Assets) |

**Why INVEST is the right word:** "Invest" is THE word for this stage. You invest in property. You invest in shares. You invest in super. You invest in yourself. The compound effect of consistent investing is how wealth is built — Einstein reportedly called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world."

---

### Stage 5: LIVE

**"Live on your terms"**

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it means** | Your wealth works for you. Passive income approaches or exceeds expenses. You make financial decisions from abundance, not scarcity. You live the life you choose. |
| **Key question** | "Can I live the life I want without financial worry?" |
| **Emotional shift** | From **building** to **freedom** |
| **Milestone** | "My passive income covers my expenses. I make choices from abundance." |
| **Measurable** | Financial Health score consistently high. Passive income growing. Debt minimal or zero. |
| **Monitrax section** | My Guide (Health, Actions, Progress) |

**Why LIVE is the destination:** "Live on your terms" is the most human, most warm destination possible. Not "financial independence" (corporate), not "retire early" (narrow), not "harvest" (agricultural). Just: live. Live the life you want. That's what financial freedom actually means.

## 3. Evidence Base & Psychology

### TRAIL Maps to Every Proven Framework

| TRAIL | Barefoot Investor | Ramsey Baby Steps | Your Money or Your Life | Maslow Financial | CFPB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Track** | Step 1: Date night | — | Step 1: Lifetime earnings | Level 1: Cash flow | — |
| **Reduce** | Step 3: Domino debts | Step 2: Debt snowball | Steps 3-6: Track + minimise | Level 1-2 | Element 1: Day-to-day control |
| **Anchor** | Step 2: Mojo bucket + Step 6: Mojo to 3 months | Steps 1+3: Emergency fund | — | Level 2: Safety | Element 2: Absorb shocks |
| **Invest** | Steps 4-5: Home + Super | Steps 4-5: Invest + education | Steps 7-8: Maximise + invest | Level 3: Accumulation | Element 3: On track |
| **Live** | Steps 7-9: Mortgage-free + legacy | Steps 6-7: Pay off home + give | Step 9: Crossover Point | Level 4-5: Freedom + Legacy | Element 4: Freedom |

### Barefoot Investor Alignment (Australian)

The Barefoot Investor (Scott Pape, 2M+ copies in Australia) uses 3 phases with 9 steps:

```
BAREFOOT:     PLANT                    →     GROW          →     HARVEST
              ─────                          ─────               ───────
TRAIL:        TRACK  →  REDUCE  →  ANCHOR  → INVEST        →     LIVE
```

### Key Psychological Principles

| Principle | Source | How TRAIL applies it |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-efficacy through small wins** | Bandura (1977) | Each stage has a clear, achievable milestone |
| **Stage-matched interventions** | Prochaska & DiClemente (1983) | Guide adapts recommendations to user's TRAIL stage |
| **Automation over willpower** | Thaler & Benartzi (2004) | Basiq auto-populates; buckets auto-categorise |
| **Financial hierarchy of needs** | Newcomb (2016), Maslow (1943) | TRAIL stages follow Maslow's financial hierarchy |
| **Behavioural momentum** | Ramsey, Barefoot | Debt snowball in REDUCE stage; smallest debts first |
| **Progressive disclosure** | NN/Group | Sidebar shows journey stages, not overwhelming feature lists |
| **Non-judgmental framing** | Financial Therapy Association | "Spending" not "Expenses"; "Debt Freedom" not "Debt Planner" |
| **Warm language** | SDT: Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness | "My Accounts", "My Wealth", "My Guide" — ownership language |
| **Action before stability** | Barefoot Step 3 before Step 6; Ramsey Step 2 before Step 3 | REDUCE (fix leaks) comes before ANCHOR (fill the bucket) |

### My Safety Net [A] — Anchor Stage

**Page:** `/dashboard/safety-net`
**Question it answers:** "If something goes wrong tomorrow, am I OK?"

**Sections:**
1. **Emergency Fund Tracker** — Visual progress bar showing months of expenses covered vs 3-month target. Calculated from liquid savings (savings + offset accounts) divided by monthly expenses.
2. **Bills & Commitments Status** — All recurring obligations with on-track/overdue indicators. Data from recurring payments + loan repayments.
3. **Safety Score (0-100)** — Weighted score: emergency fund coverage (40%), bills on time (30%), no new consumer debt (15%), positive cashflow (15%).
4. **Barefoot Mojo Tracker** — Maps user's savings accounts to the Barefoot Mojo bucket concept.
5. **Guide Recommendations** — Stage-matched advice for building the safety net.
6. **What-If Scenarios** — "What if your car breaks down ($3,000)?" — shows impact on emergency fund.

**Data sources:** All existing — `/api/master-snapshot` (balances, expenses), `/api/unified-transactions/recurring` (bills), `/api/cfo` (recommendations). No new APIs required.

## 6. Barefoot Investor Guide Integration

### Overview

The Monitrax Guide engine integrates the Barefoot Investor methodology with AI-powered personalisation. Where Barefoot stops at general advice, Monitrax's Guide uses the user's ACTUAL data to deliver personalised, stage-matched guidance.

### Stage-Matched Guide Recommendations

**Stage T (TRACK) — Guide says:**
- "Let's connect your bank and see your full picture."
- "I've found 4 accounts, 2 loans, and 156 transactions. Here's your snapshot."
- "Your net worth is $X. Let's build from here."

**Stage R (REDUCE) — Guide says:**
- "You're spending $340/month more than you earn. Let's fix that."
- "I've found 6 subscriptions totalling $189/month — want to review them?"
- "You have 4 debts totalling $28,400. Barefoot's Domino method: attack the $1,200 Afterpay first."
- "Paying $200 extra on your smallest debt clears it in 6 months."

**Stage A (ANCHOR) — Guide says:**
- "Your cashflow is positive! Time to build your safety net."
- "Your emergency fund covers 0.8 months. Barefoot recommends 3 months in a separate Mojo account."
- "Auto-transfer $400/month to your Mojo — you'll hit 3 months by October."

**Stage I (INVEST) — Guide says:**
- "Your foundation is solid. Time to grow."
- "Barefoot recommends super at 15%. You're at 11.5%. Salary sacrificing $200/month saves $1,800 in tax."
- "Your property equity grew $12,000 this quarter. LVR improved from 78% to 74%."

**Stage L (LIVE) — Guide says:**
- "Your passive income covers 62% of expenses. Target: 100%."
- "If you maintain current trajectory, you'll reach financial independence in 7.3 years."
- "Your Financial Health score is 78 — up 12 points since you started."

## 7. Design Principles

### Language Rules

| Rule | Wrong | Right |
|---|---|---|
| **Use "My" for ownership** | Portfolio | My Wealth |
| **Use human words** | Expenses, Transactions | Spending, Activity |
| **Aspiration over diagnosis** | Debt Planner | Debt Freedom |
| **Simple over complex** | Budget Analysis | Budget |
| **Progress over judgment** | You're at 45/100 | You've improved 5 points this month |

### Emotional Design

1. **Lead with the positive** — Show what users HAVE before what they OWE
2. **Normalise struggle** — "72% of people feel stressed about money. You're not alone."
3. **Celebrate small wins** — "You paid off your Afterpay! That's one less debt."
4. **Never shame** — No red alerts for low balances. Neutral tones for reality, warm tones for progress.
5. **Show the journey** — Every page reminds users which TRAIL stage they're in and what comes next

### Guidance, Not Gates

The Guide **recommends** the correct TRAIL order but does **not block** access to later stages. Users are adults — we guide, we don't gatekeep. A user in REDUCE who clicks My Wealth sees a banner: *"I notice you're looking at investments — great ambition! Want me to help optimise your cashflow first so you have more to invest?"*

## 8. Stage Progression Logic

### How Monitrax Determines Your TRAIL Stage

| Stage | Criteria to Enter | Criteria to Complete |
|---|---|---|
| **Track** | User signs up | All accounts connected. Net worth visible. Income and spending tracked for 30+ days. |
| **Reduce** | Track complete | Positive monthly cashflow for 3+ months. Total consumer debt decreasing. Budget set and adherence > 70%. |
| **Anchor** | Reduce complete | Emergency fund >= 3 months expenses. Bills paid on time for 3+ months. No new consumer debt for 3+ months. |
| **Invest** | Anchor complete | Net worth increasing for 6+ months. Active investment contributions. Property/investment portfolio growing. |
| **Live** | Invest complete | Financial Health score > 75. Passive income > 50% of expenses. Minimal or zero consumer debt. |

### Users Can Be in Multiple Stages

Financial life is not perfectly linear. A user might be in INVEST (growing investments) while still working on REDUCE (paying off a car loan). The TRAIL stage represents the user's **primary focus**, not a rigid gate.

## 9. TRAIL Check — Pre-Signup Assessment

### Overview

The TRAIL Check is a **free, pre-signup assessment** that lives on the public website at `/trail-check`. It requires no login, no bank connection, and no financial data — just 5 self-assessment questions that take ~60 seconds.

**Purpose:** Hook visitors by showing them their TRAIL stage and giving them 3 actionable tips BEFORE asking them to sign up. Value first, signup second.

**Strategy:** This is a lead generation tool disguised as a value-add. The user gets something (their TRAIL stage) before they give anything (their email). Same playbook as Credit Karma (free credit score), Calm (wellness assessment), and YNAB (free workshops).

### The Five Questions

Each question maps to a TRAIL stage. Scored 0-3 per question.

| # | Question | Maps to | Options (score) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | "How well do you know your financial picture?" | **Track** | Know exactly (3), Rough idea (2), I avoid looking (0) |
| Q2 | "At the end of each month, do you have money left over?" | **Reduce** | Yes, consistently (3), Sometimes (2), Usually run out (0) |
| Q3 | "If your car broke down ($3,000), could you cover it without borrowing?" | **Anchor** | Yes easily (3), Tight but manageable (2), Would need to borrow (0) |
| Q4 | "Are you actively growing your wealth?" | **Invest** | Yes, regularly (3), Started but inconsistent (2), Not yet (0) |
| Q5 | "How would you describe your relationship with money?" | **Live** | Confident and in control (3), Getting there (2), Overwhelmed (0) |

### Scoring Logic

```
Total score (0-15):
  0-3   → Stage T (Track)    "Let's start by seeing your full picture"
  4-6   → Stage R (Reduce)   "Time to fix the leaks and get cashflow positive"
  7-9   → Stage A (Anchor)   "Build your safety net — you're almost there"
  10-12 → Stage I (Invest)   "You're ready to grow your wealth seriously"
  13-15 → Stage L (Live)     "You're on track for financial freedom"
```

### Results Page

Shows:
1. **TRAIL stage indicator** — visual T-R-A-I-L progress bar with user's position highlighted
2. **Stage description** — what this stage means and what to focus on
3. **3 personalized tips** — stage-matched actionable advice
4. **CTA:** "Want a personal Guide to walk you through this? Start your TRAIL — it's free"
5. **Optional email capture:** "Save your results" (lead generation)

### Technical

- **Route:** `/trail-check` (public, no auth)
- **Backend:** None — all client-side scoring
- **Data persistence:** Results stored in localStorage for handoff to Guided Setup after signup
- **Design:** TRAIL-themed, same warm stone/amber palette as landing page, Framer Motion animations
- **Mobile:** Full responsive, works on all devices

### Connection to Guided Setup

When a user who completed the TRAIL Check signs up and enters the Guided Setup wizard:
1. Wizard reads TRAIL Check results from localStorage
2. Skips the Welcome step (profile already inferred)
3. Shows: "Welcome back! You're at Stage [X]. Let's set up the tools for your journey."
4. Pre-highlights the relevant sidebar section after setup completes
