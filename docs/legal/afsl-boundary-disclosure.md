# AFSL Boundary Disclosure

**Effective from:** 2026-05-24

---

## What Monitrax is

Monitrax is a **software service** that helps you organise and visualise your own personal financial information. It shows you:

- Your accounts, balances, debts, and investments
- Where your money is going
- General Australian-context information about tax, super, property, and entity structures
- Modelled "what if" scenarios so you can explore the consequences of decisions before making them

---

## What Monitrax is NOT

**Monitrax does not hold an Australian Financial Services Licence (AFSL).**

**Monitrax does not hold an Australian Credit Licence (ACL).**

**Monitrax does not hold a Tax Agent Services Act (TASA) registration.**

This means:

- We do **not** provide **personal financial advice** within the meaning of s766B(3) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). We do not take your personal circumstances into account to recommend a particular financial product or course of action.
- We do **not** provide **personal credit assistance** within the meaning of the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth).
- We do **not** provide **tax advice** within the meaning of the Tax Agent Services Act 2009 (Cth).
- We do **not** recommend specific financial products, super funds, lenders, brokers, investments, or insurance providers.

---

## What the information in Monitrax actually is

The information shown in Monitrax — including scores, projections, scenario outcomes, and benchmark comparisons — is **factual information** and **general information** only. It does not take into account your individual objectives, financial situation, or needs.

Before acting on any information in Monitrax, you should:

1. Consider whether the information is appropriate to your circumstances; and
2. If the information relates to a financial product, obtain and read the relevant Product Disclosure Statement (PDS); and
3. Consider getting advice from a licensed professional — see the next section.

---

## How to get personal advice if you need it

If you want personal financial advice, personal credit advice, or personal tax advice, you should consult a licensed professional:

- **Financial advisers** must hold or be authorised under an AFSL. Find one through ASIC's MoneySmart: [moneysmart.gov.au/financial-advice](https://moneysmart.gov.au/financial-advice/financial-advice-finder).
- **Mortgage brokers** must hold or be authorised under an ACL. Find one through MFAA or FBAA.
- **Tax agents** must be registered with the Tax Practitioners Board: [tpb.gov.au](https://www.tpb.gov.au).
- **SMSF auditors** and other regulated professionals — check ASIC's professional registers.

If Monitrax connects you to a professional through our marketplace, that professional is independently licensed and the engagement is between you and them.

---

## AI features

Monitrax includes AI-powered features. Where AI is used:

- The AI may answer questions about tax rules, super rules, your data, or general financial concepts;
- The AI uses only **fact-lookup** and **scenario-modelling** tools — it is structurally restricted from recommending a course of action or a specific product;
- AI outputs are general information; the same boundary above applies.

If an AI response in Monitrax appears to be giving you personal advice, that is a system error — please report it to **admin@monitrax.com.au** and do not act on it.

---

## Decisions are yours

Decisions you make based on information in Monitrax — including budget decisions, investment decisions, debt decisions, property decisions, and tax decisions — are your decisions, made by you. We do not direct, recommend, or control those decisions.
