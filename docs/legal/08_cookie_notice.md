# Cookie Notice

**Effective from:** 24 May 2026

This Cookie Notice explains how Monitrax uses cookies and similar technologies.

It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website or application.

Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, pixels, device identifiers, software development kits, and analytics tools.

---

## 2. Why Monitrax uses cookies

Monitrax may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- keep you signed in;
- maintain secure sessions;
- remember settings and preferences;
- protect accounts from fraud and unauthorised access;
- detect suspicious activity;
- understand technical performance;
- diagnose errors;
- improve the Service;
- support billing and subscription functionality;
- measure feature usage in a privacy-protective way.

---

## 3. Types of cookies we use

### Essential cookies

These are necessary for Monitrax to work.

They may support login, authentication, account security, session management, load balancing, and fraud prevention.

If you block essential cookies, Monitrax may not work properly.

### Preference cookies

These remember choices such as display preferences, settings, or saved configurations.

### Security cookies

These help detect suspicious activity, protect sessions, and reduce account misuse.

### Analytics and diagnostic tools

These help us understand how Monitrax performs and where errors occur.

We use analytics and diagnostics to improve the Service, not to sell personal information.

---

## 4. Advertising cookies

Monitrax does not currently use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site behavioural advertising.

If this changes, we will update this notice and obtain any consent required by law.

---

## 5. Managing cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

You can usually block, delete, or restrict cookies.

If you disable or delete cookies, some Monitrax features may not work correctly and you may need to sign in again.

---

## 6. Changes to this notice

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time.

The updated version will apply from the effective date stated in that version.
