## Part C — new findings

### MON-112 — the $500,000 retirement-exemption lifetime cap is cited to the wrong provision, in two places, differently (medium)

| Location | Renders / emits | |
|---|---|---|
| `lib/tax-engine/divisions/div152SmallBusinessConcessions.ts:149` | `// s152-310 lifetime cap` | ✗ |
| same file `:289`, `:493` | rationale *"Cumulative cap is $500,000 per s152-310"* + `AuthorityCitation{reference:'s152-310'}` (both twins) | ✗ |
| `components/tax/Div152AssessmentCard.tsx:353` | *"Lifetime cap $500,000 — s152-305"* (confirmed on screen) | ✗ |

Verified against the legislation: **s152-320 is "Meaning of CGT retirement exemption limit"** and is the provision that sets the $500,000 figure. **s152-310 is "Consequences of choice"**; **s152-305 is "Choosing the exemption"**. s152-305 remains *correct* where the card cites the concession itself (the ladder row) — the defect is specifically the **cap's** attribution.

Same class as MON-107/108, and it is a **producer** defect, not only a surface one: the engine's own `AuthorityCitation` carries the wrong reference. Fix shape under SSOT: one cited constant stated by the engine, read by the card (the MON-107/109 pattern) — the card must not hold its own citation literal at all.

### MON-113 — `FY_OPTIONS` duplicates the canonical configured-year list; the current FY goes unselectable the moment #1520 merges (high)

`app/dashboard/entities/[id]/tax/page.tsx:107` holds `const FY_OPTIONS = ['2025-26', '2024-25', '2023-24']` — a second, hand-maintained answer to "which financial years exist", while the canonical producer is `TAX_YEAR_CONFIGS` / `isTaxYearConfigured` (`taxYearConfig.ts:350`, `:380`). MON-104 built that resolver precisely so there would be one; this surface still does not read it.

Today the two agree by coincidence. **PR #1520 adds `TAX_YEAR_2026_27` to `TAX_YEAR_CONFIGS` and does not touch this file** (verified against the PR's 18-file list). On merge, FY2026-27 becomes configured yet stays unselectable here — a user cannot capture PSI or Div 152 facts for the financial year they are actually in. This is also the reason the MON-104 write-boundary leg is unreachable from the UI (§A3).

### MON-114 — "Clear" resets the form but does not withdraw the saved assessment (high)

Verified live on **both** cards (`components/tax/Div152AssessmentCard.tsx:440`, `components/tax/PsiAssessmentCard.tsx:284`): pressing **Clear** blanks the inputs and resets the tri-states client-side, but persists nothing. After a hard reload both assessments returned in full — `150000 / 150000 / 1` with PSB = Yes, and `120000 / 100 / 5000000 / 1500000` — together with their rendered outcomes.

A user who clears a mistaken assessment and navigates away still has it saved and still feeding the engine, with no indication anything remains. Actual withdrawal requires blanking every field and pressing **Save assessment** — which is how this run withdrew its fixtures (§D). Either Clear persists the cleared state, or it is relabelled as a form reset; the current affordance reads as removal and is not.
