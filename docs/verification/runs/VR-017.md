## Addendum — the fix (Code session, 2026-07-19, PR #1455)

**The §18 hypothesis is CORRECTED with executed evidence (§19.2).** There is NO separate emergency-fund producer reading gross accounts. The actual root cause: #1452's netting input was wrong for the live data topology.

- `liquidCash = grossLiquidCash − netWorth.liabilities.creditCards` — but `liabilities.creditCards` is **LOANS-only** (`calculateTotalLiabilities`, `lib/calculations/netWorthCalculator.ts:219`).
- The live Qantas card is a **`CREDIT_CARD`-typed ACCOUNT with a −$2,496 balance** (VR-007 capture:483 — one of the 3 accounts), so the subtraction was **0** and `quickMetrics.liquidCash` stayed gross ($304,304). Safety Net + the emergency months read that canonical figure faithfully — the figure itself was gross.
- Balances' $301,808 was an **accident of the buckets' `min(gross, assets.accounts)`**: the −$2,496 account balance is embedded in `assets.accounts`, so `liquidBasis` landed at the net number without any credit-card subtraction firing. (This also explains VR-010's inert cards-aware caption — its `creditCards > 0` guard reads the loans-only figure.)
- The golden passed because it modelled the card as a **Loan** — FIX_PROTOCOL §1 **F2: same engine, different inputs**, at the fixture level.

**The fix (PR #1455):** ONE canonical producer `lib/calculations/liquidCash.ts` `computeLiquidCash(accounts, creditCardLoanTotal)` — nets revolving credit in **both** representations (CREDIT_CARD loans + negative-balance CREDIT_CARD accounts). Master + the CFO emergency-buffer signal route through it; the inline netting is deleted. Ratchet: `tests/golden/ring2.liquidCashParity.accountCard.test.ts` locks the LIVE (account-typed) topology + the I9 months identity end-to-end through the real master service + safety-net route — it fails on pre-fix code. Expected on the Ring-3 re-run: **$301,808 / 11.6** on Safety Net, Home Health, Balances, /cashflow; regression guards: net worth $3,401,782 + bucket tie-out unchanged, Qantas still its own Credit −$2,496 line, tax untouched.
