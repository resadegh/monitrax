## Part D — the ten new defects

### MON-115 (high) — `aggregateExpensesByCategory` has no one-off gate

`lib/calculations/expenseAggregator.ts:133` loops over **every** expense and applies `toMonthly(expense.amount, expense.frequency)` with **no `isRecurring` check**. The sibling aggregate path applies the MON-082 one-off gate; this one was never migrated. `snapshot.expenses.monthly.byCategory` therefore carries the one-off pile, `app/api/dashboard/spending-pareto/route.ts:68` sums it, and the Expenses page renders:

> **WHERE 80% OF YOUR SPENDING GOES — $52,323 / month**

directly above its own tile reading **Total Outgoings $14,261/month** and **"132 one-offs — counted once, not monthly."** The panel total reconciles exactly to `one-offs $50,840 + recurring $1,482 = $52,322`.

This is the MON-023/037/082 defect class alive in a producer the fixes never reached — precisely the "migrate every bypassing surface in the same PR" clause of the first law.

### MON-116 (high) — emergency fund sized on a burn that includes one-off capital spend

Safety Net's **"Monthly outgoings $25,973"** (also Home's "Annual outgoings $312K") sets months-covered **11.6** and target **$155,836**. The Expenses page's own run-rate is **$14,261/mo**. On that basis the same liquid cash covers **~21 months**. Two screens use the same words for different numbers, and the one that drives the headline includes one-off outlays a buffer shouldn't be sized against. **The right basis is a decision for Reza, not a fix I should pick.**

### MON-117 (high, money consequence) — the super cap meter ignores employer SG, and the Tax page advises on the phantom headroom

| Surface | Says |
|---|---|
| Tax → Super tab | Concessional **$0** used · **"$30,000 remaining of $30,000 cap"** |
| What-If → salary sacrifice | **"Max within cap ($200/mo)"** ⇒ ~$2,400/yr headroom |
| Tax → Overview | *"You have $30,000 unused concessional cap… **Potential savings: $6,600**"* |

SG at 12% on the $227,520 salary is ~$27,302, which the What-If counts and the Super tab does not. **The Tax page is recommending a $30,000 sacrifice into roughly $2,700 of real headroom** — acting on it triggers Division 293 / excess contributions tax. User-facing money language, so this also needs the AFSL boundary check.

### MON-118 (medium) — Deduction Summary rows don't sum to their own total

`Property Expenses $172,215 + Depreciation $30,006 + Other Deductions $110 = $202,331`, printed above **Total Deductions $172,325**. Depreciation already sits inside the property figure and is listed again without an "of which" label. Expenses page **$12,779/mo** (rows sum exactly) vs Activity YTD Sankey **"Loans $106K/yr"** (~$8,833/mo) and Home's budget tile **"Loans $8,817"**. This is exactly the competing-producer split the invariant map predicted: `lib/services/loanCosts.ts:totalLoanMonthlyCost` (actuals-first, MON-081's canonical fix) against `lib/calculations/loanAggregator.ts:aggregateLoanRepayments` (raw `minRepayment`).
