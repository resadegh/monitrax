What this means

By salary-sacrificing, you're investing before-tax income, which is taxed at a flat 15% within super—typically much lower than your marginal tax rate.

This strategy reduces your taxable income today while significantly boosting the compounding engine of your retirement nest egg. The earlier you start, the more profound the effect.
